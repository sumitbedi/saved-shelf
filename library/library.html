Bring in your whole history

  1. On LinkedIn, open Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data.
  2. Choose Saved items (or the larger archive) and request it. LinkedIn emails you when it’s ready, often within minutes.
  3. Unzip it and pick the saved items file (a .csv) here.

The export has the link and the date you saved each post, not the text. Text gets added when you save posts or open your Saved page. Nothing leaves this computer.
